Aivox Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

This policy covers Aivox for Android (com.monkeycoding.aivox.callrecorder). For privacy questions, contact contact@monkeycoding.com.

What Aivox does

Aivox provides manual call recording only on explicitly validated device and firmware configurations. You choose when to start and stop a recording. Capture is disabled on unsupported devices. Aivox has no automatic call detection or recording, transcription, or AI processing in this MVP.

Recordings and controls

Completed recordings are stored locally in Aivox's app-specific storage on your device. You can play, seek, and delete them in the app. Deletion requires your confirmation. Aivox does not automatically upload recordings and has no cloud recording or backup service. Export happens only when you choose Export copy and select a destination in Android's system document interface. Once exported, the copy is handled by the destination you chose. Removing Aivox may remove its locally stored recordings, so export any files you want to keep first.

Wireless Debugging and local setup

On a supported configuration, you may voluntarily enable Android Wireless Debugging to pair Aivox with the local Android ADB service. You enter the pairing code for Android's pairing flow; Aivox does not retain it as a normal credential. The app stores its ADB identity in app storage protected by Android Keystore-backed encryption. After explicit activation, a fixed-purpose, authenticated helper runs under Android shell and communicates with Aivox over the local device. Pairing or activation may need repeating if Android revokes authorization, or after a reboot or helper stop. Aivox does not provide an arbitrary shell command interface.

Network and data sharing

Aivox uses local networking for Wireless Debugging pairing, local ADB communication, and the local helper. Opening this policy or Help in a browser requests the public pages from GitHub Pages; your browser's privacy practices apply. The app has no user accounts, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, developer-operated recording server, or automatic diagnostic upload. If you choose Share activation diagnostic, Android opens a share chooser for a setup summary including device model, firmware fingerprint, and activation events, but no pairing code, key, token, or recording. Nothing is sent to us automatically. If you email support, we receive what you choose to send and use it to answer your request.

Android permissions

Aivox requests INTERNET for local ADB networking and POST_NOTIFICATIONS for same-phone pairing interaction on supported Android versions. It does not request microphone, phone-state, call-log, broad storage, or Accessibility permissions. The fixed-purpose helper's Android shell access is disclosed during setup.

Your choices and legal responsibility

You can decline setup, disable Wireless Debugging, delete a recording after confirmation, or uninstall Aivox. Recording-consent laws vary by location. You are responsible for obtaining any legally required consent before recording a conversation.

Changes and contact

We may update this page when Aivox's behavior changes. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy or support questions, email contact@monkeycoding.com.
